How to use

1. Enter your 2026 ending balances. Sick leave shows four digits, such as 2190.
2. Each pay period, enter hours Earned and Used for each leave type. Balances update automatically. Sick leave earned starts at 4 hours and can be edited.
3. On each day, choose A, S, or C and the hours used (half hours are allowed). Holidays are marked for you. Daily hours are not copied into the Used column.
Your entries are saved in this browser only.

Annual leave earned each pay period

Less than 3 years: 4 hrs
3 – 15 years: 6 hrs (10 hrs in last pay period — type 10 on pay period 26)
15 years and over: 8 hrs
Choose 4, 6, or 8 next to Clear all entries. That fills all 26 annual earned cells, and you can still change any pay period.
