How to use

1. Enter your 2026 ending balances.
2. Each pay period, enter hours Earned and Used for each leave type. Balances update automatically.
3. Optionally note each day in the calendar cells, e.g. S-4 for 4 hours of sick leave or A-8 for a day of annual leave.
