Regular Federal Employee's Leave Record – 2027

26 pay periods this year (Jan 10, 2027 – Jan 8, 2028). Free leave chart compliments of fedretire.net

How to use

Enter your 2026 ending leave balances at the top of each leave category.
Enter leave hours used for each leave type per pay period. Accrued annual leave balances update automatically based on your selection, either 4, 6 or 8 hours per pay. All entries can be edited for part-time employees.
When taking leave, choose A, S, C1, or C2 for that day and the hours used (fractional hours are allowed). Daily hours are not copied into the Used column. You must add them to each leave category on the right side of the form per pay period. Your entries are saved automatically.
Bookmark leave.fedretire.net, or save that same address as a desktop shortcut. A shortcut to a different address, or one that opens in another browser, shows the sign-up form again. Enter the same email and your saved chart will load.

Annual leave earned each pay period

Less than 3 years: 4 hrs
3 – 15 years: 6 hrs (10 hrs in last pay period — type 10 on pay period 26)
15 years and over: 8 hrs
Choose 4, 6, or 8 next to Clear all entries. That fills all 26 annual earned cells, and you can still change any pay period.
